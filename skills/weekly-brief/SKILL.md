# Weekly Brief Review Skill

1. Read `WEEKLY_BRIEF.md` and the three JSON evidence files under `outputs/`.
2. Preserve every number exactly; never calculate a replacement.
3. Lead with the decision, effect size, uncertainty, and required validation.
4. Separate observed evidence, scenarios, and recommendations.
5. Do not describe attribution as causal impact.
6. Do not imply that the three public datasets describe the same users or company.
7. Keep the finished brief below 700 words.
8. Flag conflicts as `[VERIFY]` rather than silently resolving them.
9. Require human review before distribution.
